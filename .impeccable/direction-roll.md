## The grounded list, ordered by resonance

Derived from the audience's own world, which is UK car ownership read through a
Fenland market city and its modified-car scene, and deliberately spanning more
than one material family: regulatory signage, print ephemera, technical
publishing, instrumentation, official documents, and place vernacular.

1. The UK number plate and its regulatory typography
2. The car-meet flyer and rolling-road-day poster
3. The Haynes manual exploded diagram
4. The air-suspension management controller
5. **The MOT certificate and the stamped service history book** ← assigned, and built
6. Fenland drove-road vernacular: farm gate signage, weighbridge lettering
7. The paint depth gauge readout

Excluded from the list as the category rut: near-black with an orange glow and
a wet supercar hero, Bronze/Silver/Gold package tiers, the before/after slider
treated as the whole idea rather than as one exhibit, and the predictable
opposite, the white Swiss minimal detailing gallery.

Also excluded, to avoid repeating a world already shipped for a sibling
business: detailing chemical and car-care tin packaging, which is IronClad's.

Note on the slider: the user pinned it as a requirement, so it is binding and it
is built. What the rut excludes is letting it *be* the direction. Here it is one
plate inside a document.

## Challengers dealt, and how they were weighed

Each fused against the product before judging, on audience identification and
product clarity. The hand took the three strongest; the rest went to the
re-roll pool.

| Challenger | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Single-hue doll program | Strongest challenger, and it fuses honestly: the hero car really is midnight purple, so MKS could own one colour outright. But it hands you a colour, not a topology, and its script logotype and starburst fight the black disc mark MKS already uses. |
| Drawcord transforming cape | Its mechanism *is* the pinned slider, scaled up to become the page, which is a real argument. Lost on audience identification: matte black and gold is the exact look every detailing site already ships, and couture silk has nothing to say to a Peterborough forecourt. |
| Massin stage page | Real strength on the reviews, where a 5.0 from 42 deserves to be loud, and its inked-stamp portraits fuse well with a document world. Lost on product clarity: three quotes is thin material for a whole system, and the proof here is photographic rather than spoken. |
| Pickling brine calendar | Re-roll pool. Detailing is not a maturation process measured in days; nothing in the grammar maps. |
| Nixie laboratory counter | Re-roll pool. Neon orange on a blackened chassis lands straight back in the rut this brief has to escape. |
| Ikeda datamatics | Re-roll pool. Would bury four photographs under a data field, and MKS has no data. Strobing is also wrong for a phone-first audience. |

No factual grounds to re-roll existed. The service record carries the product's
truth (a professional worked on this car, and here is the record), its
navigation, its quiet and dense content, its states, and a plausible future
surface: a per-car job page is literally another certificate.
